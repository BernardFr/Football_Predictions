How to read these tables

  • Usual XI: the 11 players with the most minutes in the last matches (recent matches count more)
  • Most impact (top 3): change in the team's expected points and goal difference per match if the player misses the usual XI and the best backup starts
  • No impact (under 0.005 pts per match): played in recent matches, mostly outside the usual XI
  • Valuable scorers 2026-27 (goals): first goal = team's first of the match; equalizer = levels the score; go-ahead = puts the team ahead from level; combined = sum of the three. Own goals excluded
  • Goalkeepers 2026-27 (usual XI keeper, ranked among all 20 usual XI keepers): goals conceded while on the pitch; clean sheet = 60+ minutes and none conceded; rank 1 = fewest conceded per 90 / most clean sheets
  • Defenders 2026-27 (usual XI defenders, ranked among all 72 usual XI defenders): goals conceded while on the pitch; clean sheet = 60+ minutes and none conceded; rank 1 = fewest conceded per 90 / most clean sheets
  • Defenders on/off 2026-27: team goals conceded per 90 with the defender on vs. off the pitch (matches missed included). + = the team concedes less with the defender. Rank among the 19 usual XI defenders with 270+ minutes off the pitch
