Grade Weight Tuning

The grade mixes three skills: the result, the goal difference and each side's goals. Choosing the weights that give the highest grade only flatters the model: the grade is linear in the weights, so the answer is always 100% on the model's best part. The study asks a different question. With which weights does one matchweek's grade put a better forecaster ahead of a worse one most often?

Mean z

For one pair, the average weekly grade gap divided by its standard error. Averaged over the 23 pairs. Higher means fewer matchweeks rank the pair the wrong way round.

Why blends and variants

They are forecasters with known, realistic gaps in quality. The bookmakers and the variants turned out too close to the model to order, so blends drive most pairs.

Decision rule

Recommend new weights only if the 95% interval of the out-of-sample gain is above zero. It was, just: +0.02 at the low end.
